# Project knowledge map

This index covers the research through tranche 03. Use it to decide what can inform the design today and what needs more evidence before publication or launch.

## Organize the site and the research separately

| Site area | Main categories | Useful filters or routes |
|---|---|---|
| گیاهان آپارتمانی / Houseplants | Plant directory, individual profiles, plant chooser | Light tolerance, care effort, size, growth habit, animal-specific toxicity when supported |
| راهنمای نگهداری / Plant care | Care fundamentals, repotting, propagation, troubleshooting | Care topic, symptom, growing conditions |
| گل شاخه‌بریده / Cut flowers | Stem library, vase care | Flower type, color, selling unit |
| فروشگاه / Shop, later | Live plants, cut stems, arrangements, dried or preserved flowers, supplies | Arrangement form, occasion, flower ingredients, city and delivery window |

Plant names and scientific identities support all relevant areas. Keep «گل آپارتمانی» as a search route to «گیاهان آپارتمانی». A plant can appear in several collections. Keep «باکس گل» as an arrangement form and «ترحیم» as an occasion. Each can be used independently.

## What counts as fair project knowledge

Record the statement, scope, source, evidence status and intended use. A dated seller example can justify a vocabulary choice. A care statement needs a horticultural source that supports the wording and growing conditions. A project decision can guide implementation as long as it is labeled as a decision.

| Research category | What the evidence supports today | What remains open |
|---|---|---|
| **Market language and navigation** | The Sill collection directly demonstrates care and suitability routes. The Iranian reports provide seller-specific examples of light categories, arrangement names and occasions. Use them to seed bilingual labels and filters. [Seller evidence](market-research.md) | National demand, label popularity, and Tehran's representativeness. |
| **Plant identity and names** | Five seed terms have a working register. «پتوس» can lead to a golden pothos candidate in context. «برگ‌انجیری» needs multiple candidates, and «بابا آدم» remains unresolved at species level. Keep scientific treatments and trade names traceable. [First batch](research/batches/batch-01.json) · [Authority candidates](research/batches/batch-02.json) | Expert-identified examples for ambiguous labels, cultivar confirmation, and a larger reviewed name set. |
| **Care and media** | The strongest content coverage is one pothos pilot. It has bilingual draft wording for light, humidity and cat/dog toxicity, plus earlier draft watering and repotting notes. A Kew image page is a license candidate. [Pilot intake](research/batches/batch-03.json) | Exact claim locators, editorial sign-off, selected image and attribution, and care coverage beyond pothos. |
| **Search and navigation behavior** | Persian spelling variants, scientific synonyms and ambiguous queries provide useful expected results for a search test. [Initial fixtures](research/batches/batch-01.json) · [Expanded fixtures](research/batches/batch-03.json) | Actual rankings, successful disambiguation, and mobile usability with growers. |
| **Symptoms and diagnosis** | The reports support collecting growing conditions and considering several possible causes. Use this to design the intake questions. [Diagnostic structure](blueprint.md) | Condition-rich cases and reviewed cause links. |
| **Retail products and fulfillment** | The reported B133 and K133 substitution examples support product-specific policy records. Nargil's retail, aggregator and wholesale/project surfaces need separate treatment. [Seller policies](research/batches/batch-03.json) | Current checkout terms, delivery coverage and availability by seller, product and date. |
| **Wholesale and regional coverage** | The reports give dated Imam Reza online selling-unit examples and regional seller leads. These can inform the field collection form. [Wholesale intake](research/batches/batch-02.json) | Comparable stall visits at both markets, verified selling units, price trends and seasonality. |

Use `verified` for a direct project observation within the surface inspected, `reported` for a supplied research finding, and `unavailable` for a requested field or capture that could not be obtained. Keep those statuses independent of editorial approval. The earlier Sill observations are direct project records. The three research batches are report imports. Tehran-first and Persian-first are working project choices.

For the first release, use the evidence to organize the plant directory, seed bilingual search and prepare the pothos pilot. The [research roadmap](research-roadmap.md) tracks the evidence needed to expand coverage and publish.
