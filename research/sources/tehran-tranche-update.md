The governing standard remains the latest uploaded roadmap: preserve Persian-first discovery, stable identity records, claim-level care evidence, mobile-search testing, symptom evidence, commerce provenance, and the three evidence statuses `reported`, `verified`, and `unavailable`. ## Bilingual pothos pilot

The taxonomic anchor is now stable. Kew Plants of the World Online accepts **Epipremnum aureum (Linden & André) G.S.Bunting**, with stable IPNI/POWO identifier `87014-1`; Kew also records the historical name **Pothos aureus** as a synonym. citeturn309034search9turn309034search38 NC State likewise treats *Epipremnum aureum* as pothos and records **Scindapsus aureus** as a former name. citeturn309034search4

### Light wording

**Verified.** NC State says pothos “prefers bright, indirect light” but can survive for long periods under low light. citeturn309034search4 RHS similarly says Epipremnum prefers bright indirect light and will grow in some shade, but more slowly. citeturn309034search23 The Mashhad seller, by contrast, states that pothos is adaptable to low light and labels its light requirement as compatible with essentially any light. citeturn437486search0 That seller statement is evidence of **market wording**, not an authoritative care claim.

Recommended English publication wording:

> Pothos grows best in bright, indirect light. It tolerates lower light, but growth may slow and variegation may be reduced.

Recommended Persian wording:

> پتوس در نور روشن و غیرمستقیم بهترین رشد را دارد. نور کم را تحمل می‌کند، اما ممکن است رشد آن کندتر شود و ابلقی برگ‌ها کاهش پیدا کند.

Editorial status: **reviewed against RHS + NC State; suitable for publication**, provided “tolerates” is not translated as “prefers.” citeturn309034search4turn309034search23

### Humidity wording

**Verified, with nuance.** NC State advises increasing humidity with a humidifier or pebble tray, which implies that higher humidity is beneficial under dry indoor conditions. citeturn309034search4 RHS states that low air humidity increases transpiration and can increase wilting risk, while its general Epipremnum guidance describes ordinary home conditions as suitable. citeturn309034search21turn309034search43

The evidence therefore does **not** justify a categorical claim that pothos “requires high humidity.” A more defensible wording is:

> Pothos tolerates normal household humidity, but moderately higher humidity can be beneficial in very dry rooms.

Persian:

> پتوس معمولاً رطوبت معمول خانه را تحمل می‌کند، اما در هوای بسیار خشک، افزایش ملایم رطوبت می‌تواند مفید باشد.

Editorial status: **reviewed; publishable with the “beneficial, not mandatory” distinction.** citeturn309034search21turn309034search43

### Toxicity wording

**Verified for named animal species.** ASPCA identifies *Epipremnum aureum* as toxic to **dogs and cats**, attributes the effect to insoluble calcium oxalates, and lists oral irritation, drooling, vomiting and difficulty swallowing among expected signs. citeturn309034search0turn309034search7 A separate ASPCA pothos entry also lists horses, but the dog/cat pages give the cleanest apartment-pet scope. citeturn309034search3

Recommended English wording:

> Toxic if chewed or swallowed by cats and dogs. Insoluble calcium oxalates can cause painful oral irritation, drooling, vomiting and difficulty swallowing.

Recommended Persian wording:

> جویدن یا بلعیدن پتوس برای سگ‌ها و گربه‌ها سمی است. بلورهای نامحلول اگزالات کلسیم می‌توانند باعث سوزش و درد دهان، افزایش بزاق، استفراغ و دشواری بلع شوند.

This should **not** be generalized to “all animals” without species-specific evidence. citeturn309034search0

### Seller modifiers

The evidence supports three different semantic classes.

**Documented botanical/cultivar-level names:** only treat a modifier as a cultivar when an authority or horticultural reference records it in cultivar form. For example, NC State lists named Alocasia cultivars such as **'Borneo Giant'**, **'Metallica'**, and **'Calidora'**. citeturn285526search15

**Appearance descriptors:** «ابلق» means variegated in seller usage and should not automatically be converted into a formal cultivar epithet. Tarhegol’s product «مانسترا ابلق» supplies appearance language but does not, in the evidence retrieved here, establish a named cultivar. citeturn568758search4

**Trade/marketing descriptors:** «پنجه شیری» is documented by Tarhegol as a product label, but no authoritative botanical source retrieved here establishes it as a cultivar name. It should remain a local trade modifier until separately evidenced. citeturn568758search8

Seller images remain **unusable pending permission**. Kew’s *Monstera deliciosa* image page explicitly offers a CC BY 3.0 image and is therefore the safer publication asset. citeturn625415search23

### Symptom cases

The evidence base is still too thin for deterministic diagnostic rules. Nargil’s own contact page explicitly warns that serious plant diagnosis requires environmental conditions, soil, watering and detailed images. citeturn408828view4

Several useful **reported** or **source-observed** cases nevertheless emerged. Nargil’s golden-pothos page states that yellowing can have multiple causes, including fungal or bacterial problems, root rot, excessive watering and standing water; it also associates black leaf spotting with excessive watering. citeturn519405search10 NC State’s *Epipremnum aureum* profile independently notes root rot and blackened leaf margins with overwatering. citeturn519405search12

For broader houseplant symptom evidence, Nargil Q&A examples include cases where the user reports watering interval, light exposure, recent purchase or repotting, drainage, pests, or substrate condition. For example, one Spathiphyllum case records recent purchase, “medium” watering/light and rapid yellowing, while another records a spring repot into coco peat, 23–25°C room temperature, indirect light and twice-weekly watering. citeturn519405search18 Such records are useful as **anecdotal cases**, but they are not controlled diagnostic evidence.

The safe schema before rules are built is:

| Required field | Status |
|---|---|
| Symptom onset and progression | required |
| Light source, distance and duration | required |
| Watering method and interval | required |
| Drainage holes / standing water | required |
| Substrate composition | required |
| Recent move / repot / fertilizer | required |
| Root condition or odor | strongly preferred |
| Pest inspection | strongly preferred |
| Photo date and provenance | required |
| Diagnosis certainty | explicit, never implied |

The exit criterion for symptom-to-cause rules remains **not met**.

## Unresolved questions and exit-criteria status

The **care-literature pilot** is close to its exit criterion: the core pothos identity, light, humidity and dog/cat toxicity claims are reproducible and editorially tightened, and a reusable Kew image source has clear licensing. The remaining publication blocker is selecting the final asset and recording attribution text in the project data. citeturn309034search26

The **names-and-identity** exit criterion is only partially met. Stable authority identities exist for pothos, *Monstera deliciosa*, *Alocasia macrorrhizos*, *Dracaena trifasciata* and *Zamioculcas zamiifolia*, with Kew IDs `87014-1`, `87478-1`, `60444565-2`, `77164235-1` and `89402-1` respectively. citeturn309034search9turn625415search5turn285526search0turn625415search1turn625415search11 But Persian trade names still need additional expert-identified examples before species-level automatic mappings are safe.

The **search-and-navigation** exit criterion is **not met**, because no real two-device or participant test occurred. The protocol and fixture expansions are ready, but remain pending.

The **decision-support** exit criterion is **not met**. Current yellowing, wilt and leaf-spot material establishes plausible competing causes, but not sufficiently controlled cases for diagnostic rules. citeturn519405search10turn519405search18

The **commerce** exit criterion is partially met online: VIPShop substitution surfaces and Tehran delivery were recovered, the Mashhad channel was identified, and Imam Reza online units are observable. Physical, vendor-attributed, multi-visit Mahallati and Imam Reza/Khavaran sampling remains entirely pending. citeturn408828view2turn408828view3turn146277search0
