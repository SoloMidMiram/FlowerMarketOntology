## Cross-source benchmark

The 50-pair matrix preserves the ten bilingual targets, including six species and four genera. The 23 November access run reached no priority discovery surface. On 24 November, all 50 pairs received a bounded lookup. Angur and Golsetan used visible archive or glossary entries and scientific names when shown on the detail. Nargil and NetPlantClub used the recorded site search routes and query terms. Plantfinder used its visible catalog and declared plant sitemap. Where discovery used a listing, benchmark terms were compared against visible labels or detail names rather than submitted to site search.

A found result means the page supplied a source-side match at the requested precision. The ambiguous column includes common-name-only pages, conflicting identities, and targets represented by a variant whose relation remains unresolved. A not-found result applies to the inspected route and terms. It does not establish absence across the domain. Kew's synonym page and the linked GBIF detail pages presented a browser challenge. Linked World Flora Online records returned connection timeouts. The earlier project intake records the snake-plant synonym mapping, and the current observed names and linked authorities remain available for reconciliation. The sample yielded a detail for 34/50 pairs. No pair was unavailable or untested in the follow-up. Across 34 detail pages, observed markers covered care on 19, pests on 21, diseases on 15, symptoms on 8, remedies on 4, sources on 5, and dates on 19. The check counted identifiable markers. Prose and table fields that the evaluator did not capture remain unknown.

Automated success required a displayed scientific identity plus a populated care, morphology or taxonomy field from a shared read-only evaluator. Four of four in-matrix rechecks agreed on the fields compared. A fifth comparison used the off-matrix Plantfinder boxwood detail and confirmed its identity label and bibliography. Nargil commonly exposed care headings without a field value in the initial evaluator. Golsetan often exposed care without a scientific identity. These limits affect the automation result independently of discovery.

## Adapter priority

Angur has the strongest observed profile extraction and a published post sitemap for a future automation route. Golsetan is the strongest glossary and customer-language candidate, with a bounded noncommercial reuse statement. Nargil merits a second evaluator pass focused on actual care values and page-level conflicts. NetPlantClub is useful for authority links and synonym reconciliation, with linked records verified independently before acceptance. Plantfinder and Goldonyar remain candidates for horticultural fields once a larger detail sample establishes consistent coverage. Use the retail sites for seller vocabulary and suitability claims, and consult Flora of Iran within its published terms.
