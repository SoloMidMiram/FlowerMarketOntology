### Split-leaf philodendron conflict

There are **two different problems** hiding behind "split-leaf philodendron." First, *Monstera deliciosa* is an accepted species of **Monstera**, not a Philodendron. citeturn359897search3 Second, the plant long sold as *Philodendron selloum* / *Philodendron bipinnatifidum* has a genuine taxonomic-treatment history. A 2018 peer-reviewed study separated former *Philodendron* subgenus *Meconostigma* as **Thaumatophyllum**, based on molecular, morphological and cytological evidence, and published the combination *Thaumatophyllum bipinnatifidum*. citeturn190483view0turn723920view0

Kew's current POWO backbone, however, accepts **Philodendron bipinnatifidum** and treats *Thaumatophyllum bipinnatifidum* as its homotypic synonym, while explicitly noting that the latter has been accepted under an alternative taxonomy. *Philodendron selloum* is also synonymized under *P. bipinnatifidum*. citeturn657064search2turn657064search3turn657064search4 This is exactly the kind of disagreement the ontology should preserve: a canonical choice tied to an authority/version plus synonym and treatment history, rather than deleting the alternative name.

Recommended identity wording for the data layer is therefore: **“Current Kew POWO treatment: *Philodendron bipinnatifidum*; alternative modern treatment: *Thaumatophyllum bipinnatifidum*; historic horticultural name: *Philodendron selloum*.”** For *Monstera deliciosa*, keep it wholly separate despite the overlapping English/Persian "split-leaf/برگ انجیری" trade vocabulary. citeturn657064search2turn657064search3turn359897search3

The strongest consistent evidence is narrower: NC State says pothos **prefers bright indirect light** and can survive long periods in low light; RHS likewise says it likes bright light without direct sun, tolerates some shade, and warns that deep shade leads to weak growth and loss of variegation. citeturn324504search5turn324504search1turn324504search7

**Publication-safe wording:**  
**FA:** «پتوس نور روشنِ غیرمستقیم را ترجیح می‌دهد، اما بسیاری از انواع آن نور کمتر را نیز تحمل می‌کنند. نور بسیار کم می‌تواند رشد را کند کند و ابلقی برگ‌ها را کاهش دهد؛ از آفتاب مستقیم و شدید دور نگه دارید.»  
**EN:** “Pothos prefers bright, indirect light, but many forms tolerate lower light. Very low light can slow growth and reduce variegation; avoid intense direct sun.” citeturn324504search1turn324504search5

NC State suggests using a humidifier or wet-pebble tray to increase humidity, whereas RHS explicitly says epipremnums **do not require high humidity**. The Sill's current product page supplies a useful reconciliation: ordinary room humidity is sufficient, although the plant may benefit from roughly 40–60% humidity. citeturn324504search5turn324504search1turn866896search6

## Persian search and symptom evidence

No live mobile device, browser session on a handset, or user pilot was available in this research environment. Consequently, the roadmap's mobile-search exit criterion is still **`pending`**, and none of the following should be described as user testing. What can be established now is the normalization and ambiguity problem that the mobile protocol must test.

Current Iranian pages demonstrate that meaningful variants coexist in the wild: **سانسوریا / سانسوريا** appears in seller material; **پتوس / پوتوس** coexist in the same Mashhad post; **برگ انجیری / برگ‌انجیری** both occur; and legacy Latin names such as *Sansevieria trifasciata*, *Scindapsus aureus*, and *Philodendron selloum* remain important search aliases despite not being the selected current accepted names. citeturn316128search15turn369053search0turn271819view7turn475349search0turn475349search8turn657064search4

The ambiguity of **«برگ انجیری»** is not theoretical. One Nargil product surface uses Monstera listings while also giving **فیلودندرون** as another name; Nargil separately has a page for **فیلودندرون برگ انجیری / Philodendron bipinnatifidum**. citeturn271819view3turn316128search17 A search implementation that blindly maps «برگ انجیری» to a single species will therefore create false certainty.

A reproducible mobile protocol should use the same handset/browser/network state for each run, clear or isolate personalization, record locale and keyboard, capture screenshots, and run at least these fixtures: `سانسوريا`, `سانسوریا`, `برگ انجیری`, `برگ‌انجیری`, `نور کم`, `زرد شدن برگ`, `پتوس`, `پوتوس`, `Sansevieria trifasciata`, `Dracaena trifasciata`, `Philodendron selloum`, `Philodendron bipinnatifidum`, `Thaumatophyllum bipinnatifidum`, and `Monstera deliciosa`. For each query, the record should contain literal query string, Unicode-normalized query, device/browser/version, surface, expected candidate set, actual rank positions, zero-result state, ambiguity prompt, correction path, and screenshot ID. This protocol is an implementation recommendation derived from the roadmap requirements rather than a completed observation.

Normalization should convert Arabic `ي`→Persian `ی` and `ك`→`ک`, normalize spacing/half-space for retrieval, and preserve the raw query separately. It should **not** erase taxonomically meaningful distinctions after candidate retrieval. In particular, synonym expansion should produce candidate links, not textual replacement: `Sansevieria` → current *Dracaena* candidate; `Philodendron selloum` → *P. bipinnatifidum* under the current Kew backbone; and `برگ انجیری` → a ranked, potentially plural candidate set rather than one forced species. citeturn475349search0turn657064search4turn271819view3

Sarinaland supplies the requested independent regional seller outside Tehran: it states that it was established to connect customers directly with producers in **Mahallat**, lists a central greenhouse in Mahallat, and ships plant products nationally. citeturn324228search0turn324228search1 This is stronger regional-comparison evidence than treating an anonymous repost or directory entry as a seller.

Symptom evidence remains below the roadmap's launch threshold. The retrieved Nargil Monstera page says a healthy plant should not have brown/black spotting, yellowing, faded, dry, wrinkled or wilted leaves, but that statement lacks a documented causal environment for each symptom. citeturn271819view3 Nargil's contact page itself cautions that serious plant problems require examination of environmental conditions, soil, watering and detailed images. citeturn962038search2 Accordingly, simple web comments such as “my leaves yellowed” should be stored as **anecdotal symptom reports**, not diagnostic evidence, unless light exposure, watering pattern, drainage/substrate, timing, temperature and relevant pest/root observations are known.
