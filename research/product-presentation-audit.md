# Flower product presentation audit

The four batches supply starting links and earlier observations. Compare product pages with product pages and collection pages with collection pages. For live plants, compare The Sill and Nargil's golden pothos pages and use Sarinaland's Neon pothos as a different marketed variant. Nargil is an aggregation surface. ## Field checklist

| Product or surface | What to look for | Decision supported |
|---|---|---|
| Arrangement | Form, occasion, flower types, stem counts and ranges, dimensions, price unit, substitution scope, delivery window, care | Can I choose and receive a gift with the expected appearance? |
| Live plant | Common name, scientific identity, named cultivar or trade descriptor, pot inclusion and diameter, height, photo representativeness, light, watering trigger, shipping | Will this plant fit my space and what will arrive? |
| Cut stems | Trade name, color, grade, length, count per selling unit, provenance and vase care | Can I compare like quantities and handle them on arrival? |
| Listing or collection | Group label, parent path, visible facets, product card fields and primary action | Can buyers narrow the assortment before opening products? |

Record field visibility as **shown**, **not found on inspected surface**, **conditional**, or **unassessed**. Use conditional when a page requires a city, option, expansion or checkout step. A product title, image or marketing statement can supply a lead and has its own evidence scope. Record a seller's policy as stated, with its applicable channel or product. Preserve exact URLs and distinguish an inaccessible surface from an inspected omission.

## Proposed presentation

Model ingredient quantities as exact, range or unspecified. Attach substitution terms to the observed product and component. Preserve a seller's foam-care wording as retail content. Treat the current product price as a dated display in the seller's original unit.

For houseplants, put the common name, identity confidence, sold size, pot inclusion and suitability summary near the top. Follow with product-specific delivery, representative photo information, and a linked care profile. For arrangements, put form, approximate dimensions, ingredient range, and delivery selection near the order controls. Place substitution terms beside ingredients, and place vase care below the order information. Organize gifting by occasion, form and flower type as independent dimensions. For stems, place selling unit and stem count together so shoppers can compare prices.
